# Fact-Checking & Evidence Guidelines for YouTube Agents

## 1. Zero-Hallucination Policy
- **Never fabricate**:
  - Academic studies, laboratory names, or journal citations (e.g. do not invent "A 2023 Harvard study found...").
  - Quranic verses (Surah/Ayah numbers) or Hadith authenticity levels.
  - Historical timelines, legal statutes, or medical cure claims.
- If a statistic or study is referenced without a concrete verifiable source, flag it with `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` or ask the creator for their primary source.

## 2. Claim Classification Tiers
1. **[VERIFIED]**: Supported by reputable, peer-reviewed scientific consensus, official statistics, or primary documentation.
2. **[DISPUTED]**: Conflicting credible evidence exists; must be presented with scientific/historical balance.
3. **[UNSUPPORTED]**: Viral myth, colloquial hearsay, or ungrounded rumor.
4. **[OPINION / COMMENTARY]**: Subjective creator perspective; must be phrased as personal opinion, not objective fact.

## 3. Disclaimers for High-Risk Niches
- **Health / Medical / Psychology**: "This video is for educational purposes only and does not substitute for clinical mental health advice or medical diagnosis."
- **Finance / Investing**: "Not financial advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results."
