# YouTube Packaging Guidelines (Title + Thumbnail)

## The Single Click Surface Rule
The title and thumbnail operate as a single cognitive unit:
- **Rule of Thumb**: The thumbnail should visually dramatize the question or emotion; the title provides the narrative promise.
- **Never repeat words**: If the title says "The Real Reason Creators Burn Out", the thumbnail should NOT say "BURNOUT REASON". It should say "IT'S NOT THE EDITING" or show an overt visual of exhaustion.

## Length Benchmarks
- **Mobile Feed Threshold**: ~40 characters before truncation. Put the primary subject in words 1–3.
- **Desktop Search Threshold**: ~60 characters before truncation.
- **Hard Limit**: 100 characters.

## Typography on Thumbnails
- Maximum 3 to 4 words.
- Extreme contrast (light text on dark background, or dark text on bright isolated patch).
- Position away from the lower-right corner (where YouTube superimposes the video duration timestamp).
