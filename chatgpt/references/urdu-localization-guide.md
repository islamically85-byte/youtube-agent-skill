# Pakistani Urdu YouTube Localization Guide

## 1. Native Script Requirement (اردو رسم الخط)
- Pakistani Urdu must always be written in the authentic Perso-Arabic / Nastaliq Unicode script (`اردو`).
- **Never use Roman Urdu** (Urdu transliterated into English letters like *"aap kaisa ho"*) unless the creator explicitly requests Roman Urdu for a specific thumbnail or caption requirement.

## 2. Natural Pakistani Cadence vs. Mechanical Translation
- Avoid formal Sanskrit-heavy terminology typical of formal Hindi broadcasts (e.g. use `شکریہ` [Shukriya] instead of `دھنیہ واد`, `کوشش` [Koshish] instead of `پریاس`, `سوال` [Sawaal] instead of `پرشن`).
- Avoid stilted literal translations of English idioms. For example:
  - English: "Don't beat around the bush."
  - Bad Literal: "جھاڑی کے گرد مت پیٹو۔"
  - Natural Urdu: "بات کو گھما پھرا کر پیش کرنے کے بجائے سیدھے مدعے پر آئیں۔"
- Integrate common modern creator vocabulary naturally: `یوٹیوب چینل`, `ویڈیو`, `تھمب نیل`, `سبسکرائب`, `ایڈیٹنگ`.

## 3. Spoken Duration Adjustment
- Pakistani Urdu speaking cadence averages **~130 words per minute**, whereas conversational English is **~150 words per minute**.
- When translating an English script for voiceover, condense verbose phrasing so that spoken audio matches the original visual cut duration.

## 4. Mental Health & Sensitive Topics
- When discussing mental health, psychological awareness, or emotional abuse in Pakistan:
  - Maintain an empathetic, non-judgmental tone.
  - Use recognized Urdu terminology: `نفسیاتی دباؤ` (psychological stress), `جذباتی استحصال` (emotional abuse/exploitation), `ذہنی صحت` (mental health), `خود اعتمادی` (self-esteem).
  - Include supportive community guidance and standard disclaimers.
