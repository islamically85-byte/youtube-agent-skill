# yt-comment — Comment Triage & Community Strategy

The comment section is an active retention and community surface. Thoughtful creator replies in the first few hours build long-term subscriber loyalty and signal high viewer satisfaction to YouTube's recommendation system.

## Golden Rules
1. **Never post automatically**: This skill generates drafted responses. You review and post them.
2. **Reflect Authentic Voice**: Match the creator's personality, vocabulary, and tone (referenced from `channel-profile.yaml` or `voice.md`).
3. **No Manipulative Bait**: Avoid forced "like if you agree" or deceitful engagement tricks.

## Step 1: 5-Tier Intent Classification
Categorize incoming comments into meaningful piles:
- **Pile 1: High-Value Questions**: Genuine questions seeking deeper knowledge. Answering these demonstrates authority and provides inspiration for future videos.
- **Pile 2: Constructive Feedback & Counterpoints**: Thoughtful disagreements, corrections, or user experiences. Acknowledge with nuance and intellectual honesty.
- **Pile 3: Community Appreciation & Stories**: Personal testimonials from viewers. Respond with genuine warmth and specific reference to their comment.
- **Pile 4: Toxic / Moderation Concerns**: Spam links, abusive attacks, hate speech, or scam impersonators. Flag immediately for hiding or reporting.
- **Pile 5: Casual Noise**: Single emojis or generic praise ("first", "nice video"). Like or heart without wasting reply bandwidth.

## Step 2: The Pinned Comment Strategy
Select or draft ONE strategic pinned comment for the video:
- **Conversation Catalyst**: Pose an open-ended, thought-provoking question directly related to the video's central payoff.
- **Resource / Timecode Link**: Provide promised resource links, corrections, or key segment shortcuts.
- **Community Spotlight**: Pin a viewer who shared an extraordinary insight or personal breakthrough.

## Step 3: Response Drafting
Draft replies tailored to creator tone:
- Keep responses concise (1–3 sentences).
- Address the viewer by name/handle if natural.
- Avoid corporate or robotic language.
- Where appropriate, encourage viewers to watch a related upload on the channel.

## What to Hand Back
1. Breakdown of comments by intent classification.
2. Drafted replies for high-priority questions and feedback.
3. Recommended Pinned Comment with rationale.
4. Flagged moderation list (if any spam or abusive content was identified).
5. Final confirmation: **Ready to copy into Studio, or tweak?**
