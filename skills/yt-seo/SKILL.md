# yt-seo — Search & Metadata Architecture

Modern YouTube SEO is driven by **viewer intent and natural semantic language**, not archaic keyword stuffing. YouTube's recommendation engine indexes spoken audio transcripts, video descriptions, and engagement signals.

## The Reality of YouTube Tags
> **OFFICIAL YOUTUBE GUIDANCE**: Video tags play a minimal, secondary role in video discovery. They are primarily helpful for common misspellings (e.g., `Davinci vs DaVinci`, `Pytorch vs PyTorch`). Never waste hours assembling tag clouds or making inflated discovery claims about tag optimization.

## The Semantic Description Architecture (5000 Char Ceiling)
Structure the video description for maximum clarity and search indexing:

### 1. The Value Summary (First 3 Lines — Above the "Show More" Fold)
- ~150–200 characters summarizing the viewer's direct takeaway.
- Contains primary natural search terms naturally integrated without awkward grammar.
- Links to the primary promised resource or lead magnet.

### 2. Detailed Topic Overview (Lines 4–15)
- 2–3 well-written paragraphs expanding on the video's core insights.
- Written for human readers first, algorithm crawlers second.
- Answers the natural search queries people type into YouTube and Google Search.

### 3. Validated Chapters & Timestamps
- Paste chapters generated by `/yt-chapters` starting at `00:00`.
- Enables Google Search "Key Moments" indexing on search result pages.

### 4. Relevant Resources, Citations & Affiliate Disclosures
- Clear links to tools, sources, research papers, and products mentioned.
- Explicit, legal disclosures for affiliate links or sponsorships.

### 5. Targeted Hashtags (Max 3–5)
- Place 3–5 hyper-relevant hashtags at the bottom (e.g. `#PythonTutorial #WebDevelopment #ProgrammingTips`). Avoid hashtag clutter.

## What to Hand Back
1. Complete, copy-ready description formatted with proper line breaks.
2. Verified chapter timestamp block.
3. 3–5 focused hashtags.
4. Curated secondary tags (focused on misspellings and alternate phrasing).
5. Next step: **Ship it into Studio or revise links?**
